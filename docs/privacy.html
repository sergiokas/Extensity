Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 14, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Extensity (the "Extension") collects, uses, and protects any information you provide while using the Extension.

2. Data Collection

The Extension does **not** collect personal data, cookies, or any form of analytics. It only accesses the Chrome management API to read the list of installed extensions/apps and to enable/disable them on your behalf. All data stays locally within your browser.

3. Data Usage

The information retrieved from the management API is used solely to display the list of extensions and to toggle their enabled state. No data is transmitted to external servers.

4. Data Sharing

Since no personal data is collected, there is nothing to share with third parties.

5. Security

The Extension runs in the context of your Chrome browser. All operations are performed locally, and Chrome’s built‑in security mechanisms protect the Extension from malicious access.

6. Your Rights

You can uninstall the Extension at any time via chrome://extensions/. All data associated with the Extension will be removed automatically.
